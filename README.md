### Environmental Management Standards

* [ISO 14001 Environmental Management Systems](https://www.iso.org/iso-14001-environmental-management.html) - The world's leading environmental management standard, providing a framework for organizations to improve environmental performance.
* [Greenhouse Gas Protocol](https://ghgprotocol.org/) - The most widely used global standard for measuring and managing greenhouse gas emissions across organizations and value chains.
* [Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/) - Comprehensive sustainability reporting standards that help organizations disclose environmental impacts in a consistent and transparent way.
* [CDP](https://www.cdp.net/en) - A global environmental disclosure platform that enables companies, cities, and governments to report climate, water, and forest-related data.
* [ISO 14064](https://www.iso.org/standard/66453.html) - An international standard for quantifying, monitoring, reporting, and verifying greenhouse gas emissions and removals.

### Reporting, Disclosure & Sustainability Frameworks

* [Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD)](https://tnfd.global/) - A framework that helps organizations identify, assess, and disclose nature-related risks and opportunities.
* [International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB)](https://www.ifrs.org/groups/international-sustainability-standards-board/) - Develops globally consistent sustainability disclosure standards for investors and capital markets.
* [Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)](https://sciencebasedtargets.org/) - Enables companies to set emissions reduction targets aligned with climate science and global net-zero goals.
* [European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)](https://www.efrag.org/lab6) - Sustainability reporting standards supporting corporate environmental disclosures under European regulations.
* [Alliance for Water Stewardship (AWS)](https://a4ws.org/) - An internationally recognized framework and certification system for responsible water management and stewardship.
